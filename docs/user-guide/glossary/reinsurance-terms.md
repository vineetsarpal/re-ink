# Reinsurance Terms

This glossary defines common terms used in re-ink.

## Cedant

The insurer transferring risk to a reinsurer. Also called the ceding company.

## Reinsurer

The organization assuming risk from the cedant under the reinsurance contract.

## Broker

An intermediary that helps arrange the reinsurance contract between parties.

## Treaty

A reinsurance agreement that covers a class or portfolio of risks according to agreed terms.

## Facultative

Reinsurance written for an individual risk or policy.

## Quota Share

A proportional treaty where the cedant and reinsurer share premiums and losses by agreed percentages.

## Excess of Loss

A non-proportional agreement where the reinsurer responds above a retention and up to a limit.

## Retention

The amount of loss retained by the cedant before reinsurance responds.

## Limit

The maximum amount payable by the reinsurer under the contract terms.

## Premium

The amount paid for reinsurance coverage.

## Source Evidence

The page, passage, or bounding box from the source document that supports an extracted field.
